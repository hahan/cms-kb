Small Electronics Accessories Policy

This policy addresses returns of small electronic accessories such as cables, chargers, and headphones sold at checkout endcaps and register displays.

Eligibility

Small electronic accessories can be returned within 15 days of purchase with proof of purchase, in unused condition.
