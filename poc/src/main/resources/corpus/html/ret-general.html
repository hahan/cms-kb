General Merchandise Return Policy

This policy covers general merchandise such as home goods, toys, clothing, and sporting goods. It does not apply to electronics, marketplace items, or grocery.

Eligibility

General merchandise may be returned within 90 days of purchase with the original receipt. Unused items in original packaging are preferred but not required for most categories.

Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method when a receipt is presented, or as store credit when no receipt is available.
