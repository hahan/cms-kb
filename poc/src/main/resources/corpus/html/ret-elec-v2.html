Electronics Returns Policy

This policy covers returns of televisions, computers, tablets, phones, cameras, and other electronic devices purchased from Walmart, whether in-store or online.

Eligibility

Electronics may be returned within 30 days of purchase with the original receipt or proof of purchase. Items must be unopened, or include all original accessories, cables, and packaging. Items showing signs of physical damage not present at time of sale are not eligible for return.

Exceptions

High-value items without a receipt

If the item exceeds $500 in value and the customer does not have a receipt or other proof of purchase, do not process the return at the register. Escalate to a Returns Specialist for manual verification.

Restocking Fees

No restocking fee applies to electronics returned within the eligibility window in original condition.
