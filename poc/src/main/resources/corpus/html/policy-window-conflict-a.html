Electronics Accessories Return Window

This policy addresses returns of electronics accessories including cables, chargers, cases, and headphones purchased alongside or separately from a primary electronics device.

Eligibility

Electronics accessories may be returned within 30 days of purchase with proof of purchase, in unused condition with original packaging.
