Marketplace Items Return Policy

This policy covers items sold and fulfilled by third-party Marketplace sellers on Walmart.com. It does not apply to items sold and shipped by Walmart directly.

Eligibility

Marketplace items may be returned within 45 days of delivery, per the individual seller's return window, unless the item is marked as final sale. Proof of purchase is the Walmart.com order confirmation.

Process

Returns for Marketplace items are initiated through the Walmart.com order history page and are shipped back to the seller's designated return address, not to a Walmart store.
