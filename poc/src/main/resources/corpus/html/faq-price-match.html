FAQ: Price Match Policy

Q: Does Walmart match competitor prices?

A: Walmart.com matches select online competitor prices at the time of purchase for identical, in-stock items. In-store price matching is not offered.

Q: How do I request a price match?

A: Submit a price match request through the Walmart.com help form before completing checkout.
