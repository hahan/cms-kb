Electronics Returns Policy

This policy covers returns of televisions, computers, tablets, phones, cameras, and other electronic devices purchased from Walmart, whether in-store or online.

Eligibility

Electronics may be returned within 14 days of purchase with the original receipt or proof of purchase. Items must be unopened, or include all original accessories, cables, and packaging. Items showing signs of physical damage not present at time of sale are not eligible for return.

Restocking Fees

No restocking fee applies to electronics returned within the eligibility window in original condition.
