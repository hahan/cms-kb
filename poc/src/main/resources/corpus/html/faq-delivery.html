FAQ: Delivery & Shipping

Q: How long does standard shipping take?

A: Standard shipping typically takes 2-5 business days depending on the delivery address and item availability.

Q: What if my package arrives damaged?

A: Contact customer support with photos of the damage within 48 hours of delivery for a replacement or refund.
